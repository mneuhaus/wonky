CAD-ACID / ONE CATALOG, EXTENDED IN PLACE

100 zones, 476 variant cells.

Each zone tests a construction and its measured solid across its declared variants: V0–V3 for every zone, V4 and V5 where a zone declares them. Compare opens the V0 geometry and the declared-variant verdict on the CAD-Acid page.

V0Identity frame
V1Far translation
V2Exact quarter-turns
V3Skew rotation around an offset axis
V4Decimal radii (+0.025 mm), own closed form
V5Another FeatureScript idiom, same solid as V0

The transformation is applied to construction inputs, not to the finished result. Errata marked Disputed do not score for any kernel; notes are informational. The catalog is frozen separately from live scores.
