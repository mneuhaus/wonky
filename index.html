GEOMETRY LAB / RUST B-REP KERNEL

Geometry that says what it knows.

wonky is a Rust boundary-representation kernel with a FeatureScript front end. It favors exact predicates or named refusals over silent approximations, exports STEP and STL, and exposes a JSON CLI. CAD-Acid measures its output against Onshape and build123d/OCCT.
