### 0R.2 The translation fold still left 7 to 9.5 % of flush pockets refused, not 0.5 % (medium)

**Finding.** Section 0.2 said the residual was "about 0.5 %" and that a folded copy "has the words of the prism
extruded in place". The verifier measured the FS flush idiom (box z 0..B, pocket L..U moved by opPattern t = B − U,
two decimals): the far-cap words differ in **8.0 % (centred), 7.1 % (pocket from 0) and 9.5 % (random)** of cases.
`fold-residual.fs#centredFlush` and `#zeroFlush` stopped at `AmbiguousContact (stage 2, detail 7)`. The 0.48 % of
section 1.1 is the rate for the tray idiom (one sum, no translation), and the "in place" claim holds for the near
cap only (the far cap `(o + d) + t` differs from `(o + t) + d` in 4.5 % of random decimals). Both statements are
corrected above and in `docs/polygon-prism.md`.

**Root cause.** The two caps are meant to be equal, but the binary64 values that reach the F32x2 split differ in
the last one or two bits: FeatureScript's metre-to-millimetre conversion and the host sums each round. The split keeps
about 48 bits, so two binary64 values one ulp apart land on different words in a few percent of cases. The planar
arrangement then sees a vertex about 1e-14 mm off a plane, inside its resolution but not on it, and refuses by
design. F32 hid this because its grid was 2^29 times coarser.

**Alternatives measured and rejected** (`tmp/w2/integrate-fix2/snap-rate.mjs`):

- Snapping the host's binary64 cap sums to the shortest decimal within K ulps leaves 0.4 % / 1.1 % at K = 2 and
  0.04 % / 0.13 % at K = 8 (centred / random). Cancellation (18.1 − 16.59) needs a window scaled to the summands.
- More importantly, it would change the words of prism caps only. A cylinder sketched on a plane at the same noisy
  height keeps its unsnapped word, so a contact that builds today (words equal by luck of the same noise) could
  become ambiguous. That is a new regression class. Doing it everywhere would change every input word
  (`src/real.mjs` is shared by all serialization), so it was not done.

**Fix: retry the refused contact once with the prism cap moved onto the other operand's plane, within the
arrangement's own resolution, stated and labelled** (`src/kernel.mjs` `snapPrismCaps`, `src/boolean.mjs`).

- Only after the planar arrangement returned `AmbiguousContact`. Nothing that resolves without it is touched.
- One operand must be a polygon prism with its binary64 inputs (an extrusion, or a folded translation of one:
  `PRISM_INPUTS`). Its normal must be a coordinate axis exactly and its sketch x axis must have no component along
  it, so every cap vertex takes the cap origin's coordinate unchanged. The tool (second operand) is tried first;
  the target only when the tool cannot be snapped.
- A cap moves onto a plane of the other operand with the same axis (either direction) when
  `0 < |q − cap| ≤ CAP_SNAP.angularGuard × scale`: 1e-12 × max(1, the largest coordinate magnitude of both
  operands). That is the guard `kernel/ports/curved-validate.bend` `resolution` applies to a scale at least as
  large, so a snapped contact is one the arrangement could not resolve at all. It is 3e-11 mm for a 30 mm part.
- The cap origin's binary64 coordinate becomes the plane's decoded coordinate, and the prism is extruded again in
  Bend. The topology must be unchanged, the incidence audit must pass, and every vertex of a moved cap must decode
  to that coordinate exactly. Otherwise there is no snap.
- The snapped operand states `exactness: 'regularized'` with `construction.capSnap = [{cap, axis, fromMm, toMm,
  distanceMm, toleranceMm, onto}]`. The result inherits it (`regularizedSources`). The operation evidence records
  `capSnap: {refused: {reason, stage, detail}, snapped, retry}`.
- If the retry is refused too, the user gets the original refusal, and the retry's reason is in the evidence.
- Not snapped: tilted caps, side faces, and contacts between two operands without prism inputs (Boolean results).
  Those stay explicit `AmbiguousContact` refusals.
- The fold of Regression review 1 stays. It keeps the pattern-flush and Python `Pos() * Box()` cases exact (no label) whenever
  the binary64 sums agree, which is about 92 % of the cases.

**Evidence.**

  words differ in 8.046 / 7.053 / 9.452 % of cases, as verify-2 found. The largest decoded distance is **0.35 %

### 1.1 Coplanar caps reached by different sums (F finding 4.1, a regression against HEAD)

### 1.2 Generated sketch frames (F finding 4.2)

**Fix.** `prismInputs` projects `x` onto the plane orthogonal to the normal (binary64, before the split)
