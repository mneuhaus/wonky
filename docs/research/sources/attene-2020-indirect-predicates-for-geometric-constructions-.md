## What it is
DOCUMENTED: exact predicates evaluated over the *definitions* of intermediate points, not their rounded Cartesian approximations. Construction and predicate are one polynomial computation that can be filtered together. The 2020 paper develops line-line intersections in 2D, line-plane intersections in 3D, orient2d, projected orient2d, incircle and orient3d. Three-plane intersections are present in the current repository, but are not a separately developed formula section of this paper. This closes the gap between exact predicates on input floats and exact predicates on rationally constructed points. [Paper §§3–6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).

## How it works
### Representation and denominator discipline
DOCUMENTED: store an implicit point as its defining explicit points or equivalent homogeneous polynomials H=(Nx,Ny,Nz,W), with Cartesian coordinates (Nx/W,Ny/W,Nz/W). Predicate evaluation substitutes these rational expressions, clears denominators, evaluates the polynomial numerator, and applies the signs of denominator factors with odd multiplicity. Exact W=0 means the point is undefined, not merely a predicate's geometrical zero. A filter whose enclosure for W contains zero must defer, not declare the construction undefined. [Paper §§4,5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).

**LPI formula, DOCUMENTED [§4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2):** for line q1,q2 and plane r,s,t, let d=det(q1−q2,s−r,t−r), n=det(q1−r,s−r,t−r), and N=d·q1+n·(q2−q1). Then H=(N,d). This preserves exact rationals without any division during topological classification. d=0 covers a parallel line, coincident line endpoints, or a degenerate defining plane; topology code must distinguish meaningful cases if needed.

**TPI extension, INFERRED mathematical formulation, corroborated by current lambda3d_TPI:** for planes ai·x=bi, let W=det(a1,a2,a3) and N=b1(a2×a3)+b2(a3×a1)+b3(a1×a2). Then x=N/W for W≠0. When ai are cross products of original triangle edges and bi=ai·vi, W has coordinate degree 6 and N degree 7. Avoid first rounding ai/bi if the intended exact geometry is defined by the original triangles. [Actual homogeneous TPI implementation](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/blob/74960591e0381f85d94bcee603829451f016b4a2/include/implicit_point.hpp#L98-L143).

**Useful no-division predicate identities, INFERRED from [§4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2):**
- Against plane a·x=b, sign is sign(a·N−bW)·sign(W).
- Compare two x coordinates using sign(N1x·W2−N2x·W1)·sign(W1W2).
- A homogeneous 4×4 orientation determinant with rows (Nx,Ny,Nz,W), divided by W1W2W3W4, gives the Cartesian homogeneous determinant. Match its argument/order convention to wonky's existing orient3d, rather than assuming every library uses the same sign.
- Canonicalize a certified-negative W by negating every component. Normalizing W to positive eliminates many later sign products, but only when its sign is actually certified. Multiplying by a common nonzero scale is not permission to forget its sign.

### Filter stages and specialization
DOCUMENTED [paper §§5–6 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2):
1. Semi-static floating filter: certify every denominator sign first, then the numerator. The bound is delta(1)·B(inputs); cached implementation overbounds B by beta^k, where beta is the maximum relevant input/difference magnitude and k the total degree. Cached construction data include lambdas, W, and the magnitude needed to account for construction error. It is unsound to filter the final predicate as if the cached lambdas were exact float inputs.
2. Interval stage: reevaluate with directed-rounding intervals, including construction error; exclude zero to certify signs. The paper sets CPU rounding toward +infinity and restores round-to-nearest afterward.
3. Exact expansion stage: evaluate polynomial numerator and denominators with unbounded-length-in-principle floating expansions; exact zero is now meaningful.
4. Generate separately specialized E/I combinations. For orient2d, permutation symmetry reduces eight configurations to EEE, IEE, IIE, III; odd permutations negate the result. Do not make every explicit point an expensive implicit one just to simplify dispatch.
5. Cache float and interval construction data when a point is reused; exact expansions usually cost more memory than their rare reuse warrants.

**Current repository differs from the paper, DOCUMENTED:** generic points now include SSI, LPI, TPI, linear combinations, barycentric triangle points and tetrahedral barycenters. Constructors accept `genericPoint` references, allowing cascades; lambda routines operate on homogeneous inputs. `include/indirect_predicates.h` contains interval, expansion and bigfloat templates, but inspected public indirect wrappers such as dotProductSign2D_IEE call **interval then bigfloat**, not the paper's three-stage sequence. Expansion-template code checks FE_UNDERFLOW/FE_OVERFLOW and signals a sentinel. Ordinary orient2d/orient3d also have hand-optimized binary64 filters. Therefore distinguish historical benchmark algorithms from today's code. [Types/API](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/blob/74960591e0381f85d94bcee603829451f016b4a2/include/implicit_point.h), [generated code](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/blob/74960591e0381f85d94bcee603829451f016b4a2/include/indirect_predicates.h), [hand optimized](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/blob/74960591e0381f85d94bcee603829451f016b4a2/include/hand_optimized_predicates.hpp).

## Robustness and guarantees
- DOCUMENTED: correctness is relative to the original explicit floating inputs and exact rational constructions. Symbolic perturbation/general-position handling is separate. A nonzero denominator, exact numerator sign, and a consistent sign convention are all necessary. [Paper §§2.2,4,5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).
- DOCUMENTED: the paper only partly addresses general exact construction: irrational constructions and large cascaded algebraic histories are explicitly outside the efficient class. It gives 3D constrained Delaunay Steiner-point generation and dimensional flow complexes as examples. [§8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).
- DOCUMENTED: combinatorially correct output can acquire self-intersections when implicit vertices are rounded for a consumer; the paper explicitly calls out 3D snap rounding as unresolved in its implementation. Exact topology is not a certificate that an exported F32 mesh has a valid embedding. [§8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).
- INFERRED: replacing binary64 by binary32 or FTZ breaks existing filter constants, interval assumptions and expansion range guarantees. Bend has neither the reference's mutable rounding mode nor floating exception flags. Use an RN-only proved interval/filter layer with explicit range status, and exact U32 fallback. [Paper §5 and current build contract](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/blob/74960591e0381f85d94bcee603829451f016b4a2/CMakeLists.txt).

## Parallelism and performance
DOCUMENTED: Windows 10, Visual C++ 2019, i7-4770, 16 GB RAM. One million points with 100% implicit inputs: random 2D triangulation 3.81999 s vs CGAL exact construction 15.9442 s; projected random 3D 4.29742 s vs 50.1841 s. Authors summarize 3.86–11.7× speedups and up to 3.5× less memory, **not a universal orders-of-magnitude claim**. Explicit-only case favored CGAL: 1.168691 s vs 1.79624 s in 2D. Regular 100%-implicit projected 3D took 75.0639 s; corresponding CGAL run is reported as crashed. [Paper Tables 1–5, §7.8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).

DOCUMENTED caching result, million-point 100%-implicit random 2D: no cache 7.16629 s / 413.65 MB; FP cache 6.9816 s / 445.84 MB; FP+interval cache 3.81999 s / 509.93 MB; caching exact too 3.9589 s / 1167.84 MB. This supports selective caching, not indiscriminate exact memoization. [Table 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2).

INFERRED: independent minors and points fit fork-join; shared mutable caches and rounding-mode mutation do not. Use immutable per-batch cached homogeneous values/certificates. Group by predicate/construction type and limb capacity; filter pass first, then compact unresolved jobs for uniform exact GPU work. Dynamic nested construction histories and adaptive expansions would otherwise cause nonuniform work.

## Known failures, limitations, war stories

Reporter gives 233,200 wrong signs out of 355,200 queries in a stress case and six inverted tets in a downstream arrangement. These are reported measurements, not reproduced here. Crucially, the incorrect signs were permutation-consistent, so antisymmetry tests alone missed them. - DOCUMENTED: [PR 14](https://github.com/MarcoAttene/Indirect_Predicates/pull/14), merged August 5, 2026, fixes exact Cartesian accessors returning homogeneous numerators without dividing by W; only cascaded implicit inputs exposed it because explicit W=1 hid the error.

## Relevance for wonky
INFERRED from [the substitution scheme](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.09772v2):
1. Extend the existing indirect line-plane path with immutable LPI/TPI records referencing original primitive IDs. Exact sign evaluation and construction provenance belong together; never materialize rounded coordinates before topology classification.
2. Begin with bounded-depth, fixed-degree classes. LPI from explicit points has numerator degree 4 and denominator degree 3; TPI from explicit triangles has 7 and 6. Their side tests against an explicit triangle plane have degree 6 and 9 respectively. Generated degree and coefficient-growth metadata can determine fixed U32 limb capacities. These degree counts are derived, not paper-prescribed storage sizes.
3. **Degree alone does not determine limb count.** Also bound input significand/exponent spread or quantize to a documented lattice. Every finite binary32 can be scaled by 2^149 to an integer of magnitude <2^277; an exact sum of two such words may need 278 magnitude bits. For a homogeneous degree-d polynomial on B-bit integers, coefficient absolute sum C gives a conservative output-size bound near dB+ceil(log2 C), with separate sign and temporary growth. Prove bounds per expression and include differences/carries. Local restricted domains may be dramatically smaller than full-range F32 support.
4. Implement U32 multiplication using bounded partial products/carries, not an assumed U64 intermediate. Fixed arrays and fixed loop depth fit GPU uniformity; a silently overflowing final carry must fail. F32x2 can improve filter acceptance but cannot replace the exact fallback.
5. Exact implicit points are excellent for tagged-mesh Boolean topology, planar arrangements, vertex ordering/coincidence, and provenance-aware diff. They do not directly solve irrational cylinder-cylinder SSI or rolling-ball fillets. Generalizing to algebraic roots requires a different certified representation.
6. Keep rendering/FDM mesh coordinates as explicitly approximate views of exact implicit points, with deviation and embedding checks. Return distinct states for certified sign, exact zero, unresolved filter, undefined construction, and capacity/range failure. These are useful LLM diagnostics, not hidden epsilon fallbacks.
