## What it is
DOCUMENTED: a localized exact polygon-mesh intersection framework supporting Boolean combinations and outer-hull/self-intersection repair. An adaptive octree isolates affected regions; a plane-based BSP solves each region; exact clipping and connectivity reconstruction join those results to untouched mesh regions. The numerical contribution is avoiding repeated coordinate construction: new vertices remain intersections of existing planes, so predicate precision can be bounded in advance instead of growing with every split. This is neither a sampled voxel Boolean nor an analytic curved-surface kernel. [Paper §§1,3–6](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

## How it works
### Plane-based geometry and the actual precision bound
DOCUMENTED [§§3.1–3.3](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf): a polygon stores a supporting plane and a circularly ordered list of bounding planes. Each bounding plane passes through an original polygon edge and an additional point outside the supporting plane. Edges and vertices are implicit plane intersections. Splits add plane references, not rounded Cartesian intersections. Nonplanar input polygons must first be triangulated. Predicates test plane coincidence/co-orientation, the side of a plane containing a three-plane intersection, and whether a triple has a unique point intersection.

The input premise is an L-bit **uniform coordinate lattice**, including sign: 2^L uniformly spaced positions along each bounding-box axis. If edge-coordinate differences need K signed bits, relative maximum edge length obeys delta <= 2^(K−1−L). Construct the support plane using edge differences: n=(p1−p0)×(p2−p0), d=−det(p0,p1−p0,p2−p0), so n·x+d=0. A conservative exact coefficient budget is

M=(L−1)+2(K−1)+3+1=L+2K+1, or M >= 3L+2 log2(delta)+3.

The +3 bounds summation of six triple products; +1 counts a sign bit in the paper's conservative accounting. Its prototype embeds plane coefficients in binary64 (M=53); L=20 then permits delta<=2^-5. With extended x86 precision M=64, L=22 permits delta<=2^-2.5 and L=24 permits delta<=2^-5.5. [§3.2, pp.3–4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

**Critical distinction, DOCUMENTED/INFERRED:** M is the precision needed to construct/store the *plane coefficients exactly*. It does NOT assert that a determinant involving several such planes can be evaluated exactly in a single M-bit float. The paper explicitly uses filtered exact predicates, and §7 says the prototype uses single-stage filtering. The task hint's formula is valuable but is not, by itself, a limb budget for the whole Boolean. Also enforce the actual lattice premise: the later wording 'full IEEE 754 single precision input' must not be read as covering arbitrary binary32 exponent spreads with only 24 uniform-grid bits. [§§3.1–3.2,7](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

### Concrete determinant predicates and a Bend limb-budget derivation
INFERRED implementation formulas from the plane representation above, not additional theorems claimed by the paper:
- For three planes n_i·x+d_i=0, let W=det(n1,n2,n3) and N=−d1(n2×n3)−d2(n3×n1)−d3(n1×n2). The unique point is x=N/W only when W!=0. W=0 needs degeneracy handling; it does not by itself distinguish a common line from inconsistent planes.
- For a fourth plane, Q=n4·N+d4 W equals the 4×4 determinant whose rows are (n_i,d_i). Its side at x is sign(Q)·sign(W). Do not discard the denominator sign. If Q=0 with W!=0, the point is exactly on the plane.
- Equality of two finite implicit points (N,W) and (N',W') can be tested by the three exact equations N_j W'−N'_j W=0. Those comparison polynomials need their own capacity proof; the plane-side budget below does not automatically cover every downstream operation.
- Coincident plane equations are proportional homogeneous four-vectors; exact 2×2 minors establish proportionality, followed by a nonzero normal-component or exact normal-dot-product sign to distinguish co-orientation. Reject a zero normal as an invalid supporting plane rather than treating it as ordinary coplanarity.

For integer-scaled coefficients with |n_j|<2^Bn and |d|<2^Bd, a deliberately conservative bound is |W|<6·2^(3Bn)<2^(3Bn+3) and |Q|<24·2^(3Bn+Bd)<2^(3Bn+Bd+5). Thus sign-magnitude storage can use ceil((3Bn+3)/32) U32 magnitude limbs for W and ceil((3Bn+Bd+5)/32) for Q, with a separate sign and enough carry storage at each implementation step. If every coefficient has the same B-bit magnitude bound, Q needs at most 4B+5 magnitude bits. These are derived upper bounds, not measurements or code capacities from the paper. [Representation and finite-precision premise](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

Example, INFERRED: L=20, K=16 satisfies the paper's delta=2^-5 case. Edge-difference construction gives Bn=2K−1=31 and Bd=L+2K=52. This particular four-input-plane side test fits 150 magnitude bits, i.e. five U32 limbs, despite exact plane coefficients fitting the paper's M=53. It is NOT a proof that five limbs suffice for the entire algorithm: include auxiliary edge planes, octree boundary planes, all point comparisons and each rescaling. Clearing dyadic denominators changes integer widths even though it does not change the geometric plane. Compile and check those bounds per expression.

### Localized topology pipeline
DOCUMENTED [§§4–5](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf):
1. Build disjoint convex critical cells covering all intersection curves in their interiors. Refine an octree where an intersection may occur and more than m polygons cross a cell. m=17 minimized runtimes in most reported experiments; it is a tuning observation, not a universal bound on degeneracy or work. Put axis-aligned cell boundaries between possible input vertex coordinates to avoid vertices/edges/faces lying exactly on boundaries.
2. Use conservative polygon/cell overlap tests: enlarged cells and false positives are allowed, false negatives are not. For ordinary Booleans, different input meshes visiting a cell are already a conservative refinement criterion. Self-intersections need actual within-mesh detection; the authors use a spatial-hashing variant with exact triangle-triangle tests.
3. Clip incident polygons to each critical cell using only the plane-based split routine. Build local BSPs by recursively inserting polygons and splitting along their planes. For solid Booleans, label leaves inside/outside using orientation, merge BSPs and apply union/intersection/difference truth tables to leaf labels.
4. Extract boundaries by propagating a node's splitting-plane polygon through its subtrees. Keep fragments separating inside and outside cells. This initially yields polygon fragments, not automatically a connected halfedge mesh.
5. In the original halfedge mesh, associate bounding planes with halfedges, clip against each critical cell's six planes and retain the outside fragments. Preserve links from every extracted fragment back to its original polygon.
6. Establish local vertex coincidence inside a small cell with exact plane predicates. Globally, restrict matching to fragments sharing an original input polygon using the provenance links. The special cell placement avoids needing unrestricted cross-original-edge searches at the stitch boundary.
7. Repair T-junctions without geometric snapping: identify collinear halfedge loops, sort their implicit T-vertices exactly along the common line, and insert matching vertices into opposite edges. The paper allows inserting a 180-degree polygon corner or splitting that polygon. Only then materialize Cartesian coordinates to the required output precision.
8. Classify untouched/unconnected components, including fully nested or nonintersecting solids, by octree-accelerated ray shooting. Simply deleting every component that never visited an intersection cell would give the wrong Boolean.

For self-intersecting/nonoriented surfaces, initial BSP leaves cannot simply be called inside/outside. Build the BSP-cell adjacency graph, remove graph edges crossing the input surface, and flood-fill connected volume components. Extract candidate component boundaries and decide globally which form the desired hull. The ordinary outer hull is the boundary of volume unreachable from infinity; the orientation-sensitive extension removes compatible, oppositely oriented coincident patches so splitting/merging fronts can be represented. This hull interpretation deliberately ignores internal voids. [§§4.2,6, Figures 3,5,6](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

## Robustness and guarantees
- DOCUMENTED: the geometric calculations during plane-based topology processing are exact under the finite input/coefficient contract, with no reconstructed coordinates feeding later predicates. Precision is bounded because all relevant planes originate in the input or the controlled spatial partition, not recursively rounded output geometry. The paper delegates predicate/splitting machinery to Bernstein–Fussell and Shewchuk; it does not publish a self-contained implementation or full list of machine-sized determinant bounds. [§§3–4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- DOCUMENTED: quantization and/or splitting long input edges can enforce the coefficient condition. Quantization introduces geometric displacement; the paper quotes errors on the order of 2^-20 relative to the bounding box for one configuration. INFERRED: preserving the input mesh's combinatorial connectivity is not a proof that quantization preserves every narrow gap, coincidence or contact in the original embedding. Require an explicit modeling tolerance and keep the original/quantized distinction visible. [§3.2](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- DOCUMENTED: **final rounding can create microscopic self-intersections** even after exact topology processing. This is explicitly identified as the geometric-rounding problem. Exact implicit topology alone is therefore not a valid-F32-output or printable-mesh certificate. [§4.3, p.6](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- DOCUMENTED: touching degeneracies may produce nonmanifold edges or vertices. The scheme handles them, but a consumer demanding a manifold mesh must choose a topological merge/separation policy. This is a semantic decision, not permission to nudge coordinates arbitrarily. [§7, p.9](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- INFERRED: paper-level exactness does not supply an FTZ-safe floating implementation. Bounds on significand length do not replace exponent/range bounds. U32 integer evaluation on an explicit lattice avoids that issue for the exact tier; an F32 fast filter still needs proven overflow/underflow handling. Substituting F32x2 for M=53 is not exact plane conversion: bounded-error double-word arithmetic is not a 53-bit exact integer container. [Paper §3.2](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf), [double-word arithmetic assumptions](https://hal.science/hal-01351529v3/document).

## Parallelism and performance
DOCUMENTED, historical CPU results [§7 / Figure 7](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf): Intel Core i7 2.67 GHz, 6 GB RAM. For the 30-degree Iphigenie intersection, whole-pipeline times were 6.9 s versus CGAL 17.6 s at 25K faces, 19.1 s versus 113.9 s at 200K, and 95.8 s at 3.2M (CGAL unavailable at that size). The authors report measured speedups 2.5–13×; their approximately 25× large-input claim is explicitly extrapolation, not a run. CGAL's internal representation already used about 5.3 GB near the accessible size limit; their method used less than 300 MB in the reported comparison. Do not compare these 2010 numbers directly to today's kernels.

DOCUMENTED validation/results: round-trip conversion of 25 meshes, 36 to 1M faces, gave zero measured vertex-set Hausdorff distance. Subtracting meshes from copies shifted by one representable coordinate step produced closed manifold results agreeing with their CGAL reference at input precision. Outer-hull repair took 1.2 s for Cow (6K faces) and 1.9 s for Woman (12K). Self-intersection-free front-tracking checks cost about 100 ms for 15K, 300 ms for 50K and 1600 ms for 400K faces; a 400K case with nine small intersections took 5.2 s overall. These are paper-reported observations, not local reproduction or universal correctness tests. [§7](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

INFERRED: independent critical cells and predicate batches fit Bend fork-join well; determinants have fixed expression trees once limb widths are fixed. The paper's near-O(sqrt(n)) local work comes from roughly uniform sampling and a small critical region; octree construction and final joining remain additional costs, and thin/large polygons spanning many cells increase overhead. BSP insertion, flood fill, halfedge stitching and adaptive octree depth are irregular and mutable in their conventional form. A Bend port needs immutable fragments, index-based relations, sorted joins/component passes and explicit work queues. m=17 cannot be treated as a proof that every GPU job has identical bounded work; use workload buckets/capacity checks, route overflow to another bounded Bend batch, or fail explicitly. [§§4.1–4.3](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

## Known failures, limitations, war stories
- DOCUMENTED: rounding to the consumer's coordinate format can reintroduce self-intersections; coplanar fragmentation may remain and requires exact coplanar simplification; long/poor-aspect triangles can defeat localization efficiency. [§§4.1,4.3](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- DOCUMENTED: the outer-hull definition ignores internal voids; creating new voids is listed as future work. It is not a drop-in cavity-preserving solid-healing operation. [§§6.1,8](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).
- DOCUMENTED: the only official interactive service found is discontinued, with no replacement/source link on its status page. No canonical issue tracker was identified; do not manufacture repository failure history. [WebBSP](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/webbsp).
- INFERRED limitation relevant to repeated CSG: keeping the original plane representation prevents construction-depth growth, but exporting rounded coordinates and then reimporting/rebuilding support planes abandons that guarantee. New modeling primitives or transformations need fresh verified coefficient budgets. [§3.1 invariant](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf).

## Relevance for wonky
INFERRED from the [plane-based invariant, conversion analysis and localization](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/campen_2010_eg_021.pdf):
1. Strong architectural fit for the EMBER-style planar Boolean path and for rational topology in tagged-mesh/analytic-recovery hybrids. Store immutable plane IDs and triple-plane vertex definitions with provenance; evaluate exact classification before approximate coordinate recovery. It does not solve plane-cylinder/cylinder-cylinder SSI or rolling-ball fillets.
2. Prefer a **bounded-input exact U32 kernel**: choose and document a lattice/exponent contract, construct planes exactly in limbs, certify per-predicate bit growth, and run F32 filters only as an optimization. U32 multiplication needs partial products/carries, e.g. split words into 16-bit halves with explicit carry handling; no hidden U64 intermediate. Fixed arrays and fixed-depth limb kernels satisfy no mutation/FFI requirements.
3. The algebraic degrees stay fixed while splitting existing planes, but the topology algorithm is not automatically GPU-uniform. Port numeric predicates first; independently assess a pure persistent local-BSP/fragment pipeline rather than promising a direct code translation.
4. Quantization is an optional, explicitly bounded approximation, not a hidden Boolean tolerance. An exact dyadic representation of arbitrary F32 inputs is also possible but has a much larger exponent-derived limb budget than an L=20 normalized lattice. F32x2 remains a filter/evaluation tier, not the exact plane store.
5. For FDM, verify the rounded output embedding/watertightness and deviation after mesh extraction. Preserve implicit exact points in the model so render/export coordinates are replaceable approximations. Nonmanifold contact policies and internal-cavity preservation must be explicit.
6. Provenance links used for efficient stitching also support stable naming and meaningful diff: retain source face/plane IDs across fragments, but do not confuse coincident geometry with semantic identity. Expose failures such as degenerate plane, nonunique triple, insufficient bit budget, unresolved component and unsafe output rounding in LLM-facing diagnostics.
7. Prototype checklist (not run): det3/det4 sign conventions and denominator sign; exact coincident planes/triples; lattice limits and longest edges; clipping/stitching at critical-cell boundaries; T-junction ordering; contained/disjoint components; high-valence/coincident-face workloads; and one-representable-step shifts. Cross-check the fixed-limb result against a separate exact oracle before optimizing filters.

## Verdict: adapt
Adopt the plane-only construction invariant and explicit precision budgeting; adapt localization, topology assembly and exact arithmetic to immutable Bend. Do not mistake M>=3L+2log2(delta)+3 for the complete determinant budget or assume exact topology survives coordinate rounding. Paper reachable and useful despite the dead service. Open work: complete per-predicate/all-plane limb proofs, pure topology implementation, FTZ-safe filters and rounded-output certification. No production files changed.
