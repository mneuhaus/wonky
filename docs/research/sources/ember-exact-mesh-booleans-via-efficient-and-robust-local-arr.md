## What it is

DOCUMENTED: exact Booleans on a polygon soup using fixed-width integer plane geometry, per-polygon local BSP arrangements and winding-number classification. Rather than construct a global arrangement/acceleration structure first, recursively subdivide space, carry a local reference point with known winding vector, solve small leaves, and prune regions that cannot contribute. Handles piecewise-constant integer winding-number (PWN) inputs, including applicable self-intersecting, non-manifold and degenerate configurations. It is **not** an exact curved B-rep Boolean or a general open-surface solid definition. [Paper §§1, 3.4, 4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

INFERRED for this research topic: two particularly valuable assets are the **bounded arithmetic contract** for exact-predicate tests and the **1000 reproducible mesh-pair transforms** for bake-off fairness. Neither alone supplies a semantic golden-result corpus.

## How it works

### Plane-based data model and exact predicates

DOCUMENTED §3: input vertices have 26-bit integer coordinates, or polygons are reused directly from previous exact results. A plane is integer `(a,b,c,d)` for `a*x+b*y+c*z+d=0`; normals need not be normalized. Convex polygon = supporting plane plus ordered edge planes; vertices are three-plane intersections. Lines are plane pairs, rays add one limiting plane, segments add two. Parallel normals satisfy `np × nq = 0`. Three-plane intersection uses signed determinant minors/Cramer's rule to form homogeneous `X=(X1,X2,X3,X4)`. `X4=0` means no unique finite intersection. Classification against plane S is `sign(dot(X,S))*sign(X4)`. Retaining the denominator sign is essential. A point belongs to a convex polygon iff support classification is zero and every edge-plane classification is <=0; strict <0 gives its interior. [Paper equations (1)–(4), §3.1](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

DOCUMENTED all necessary intermediates are bounded by **256 bits under this input/operation contract**. This is not an arbitrary-precision rational system: §3.2 explicitly explains that subtracting arbitrary homogeneous points, taking their midpoint, constructing their direct segment, or triangulating arbitrary output polygons can require larger denominators and leave the fixed-width domain. Generated points must retain defining planes; do not reconstruct new support planes from rounded generated Cartesian vertices. [Paper pp. 3–4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

### Where the bit bound comes from

DOCUMENTED EMBER cites the arithmetic foundation in Nehring-Wirxel/Trettner/Kobbelt, [Fast Exact Booleans for Iterated CSG using Octree-Embedded BSPs, CAD 135 (2021), 103015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2021.103015). I additionally read **pp. 5–6, equations (4)–(9)** of that predecessor, not its complete implementation. Let V bound absolute vertex coordinates, N bound absolute normal components, D bound plane offsets; `D <= 3*V*N`. Hadamard gives `|det4| <= 16*N^3*D <= 48*N^4*V`. For signed b-bit arithmetic require `N^4*V <= 2^(b-1)/48`. With triangle normals from edge cross-products, `N <= 8*V^2`, giving `V^9 <= 2^(b-1)/(48*8^4)` and approximately `V <= 0.239*2^(b/9)`. At 256 bits the paper gives V below about 8.73e7, slightly above 26 bits.

INFERRED Bend translation: 256-bit containers require **eight U32 limbs**, 192-bit six and 128-bit four. This is a bound on the specified expressions and input plane palette, **not permission to do general 256×256 multiplication and truncate**. Keep signed representation, carry/borrow, determinant intermediate widths and newly introduced offset planes under explicit range contracts; during verification use wider scratch limbs and assert discarded high limbs/sign extension are valid. Native x64 speed in the predecessor relies on 64-bit `adc` and 64×64→128 `mul`, unavailable as such in Bend. Build U32 carry logic and 16-bit partial-product multiplication, then remeasure. F32x2 is not a replacement for these exact integer predicates.

### Winding semantics and arrangements

DOCUMENTED §3.4: a non-surface point has integer winding vector `w in Z^n`. Each polygon carries a winding-number transition vector Δw, usually a unit vector for its source mesh. For nondegenerate segment crossings from x to y, `w(y)=w(x)+Σ sign(n_t·(x-y))*Δw_t`. Points on surfaces require front/back winding values and special handling of the polygons containing the endpoint. Degenerate paths touching edges or lying in polygons are detected and retried, not resolved by a floating epsilon.

A Boolean is an indicator `f(w)`; union is IN if any component is nonzero, while difference/intersection use appropriate combinations. Split polygons until their entire interiors have constant front/back winding pair `(wF,wB)`. Keep OUT→IN, reverse IN→OUT, discard IN→IN and OUT→OUT. Thus geometric subdivision and Boolean selection are separate. [Paper equations (5), §3.4–4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

### Recursive computation, concrete implementation order

DOCUMENTED §§4.1–4.5:

1. Root task has convex polygons with Δw, integer AABB, reference point and known winding. Split along an axis-aligned integer plane. Clip each polygon by classifying its vertices -1/0/+1; vertices crossing -1↔+1 are exact intersections of support, edge and split planes. All-coplanar polygons belong to the **left** child only, preventing duplicate counting.
2. Each child needs a valid reference point inside it. Retain the old one when possible; otherwise project it to the child's AABB and trace winding from old to new. This keeps all subsequent classification local. Invalid edge-touching/copolanar trace paths require another valid path.
3. At a small leaf, for each polygon t build a temporary **local BSP of t**. Intersect it with other leaf polygons; no intersection and isolated point contact require no split, proper intersection segments are inserted as BSP cuts, coplanar overlaps add the other polygon's edge cuts. Keep cells convex even if a split extends beyond the actual segment.
4. For coplanar overlapping patches, use a total input-polygon order and disable duplicate overlap cells except the lowest-index owner. Winding still accounts for all source layers. Test this rule together with winding multiplicity; dropping duplicate geometry alone would lose semantics.
5. Classify each retained leaf cell via an interior point and segment traces to the reference. Fast proposal: rounded approximate centroid, axis-aligned line least parallel to the support, exact intersection, then exact validity tests. Fallback moves two incident edge planes by positive integer offsets, intersects with the supporting plane, and retries until interior.
6. A direct segment between two homogeneous points may not fit the numeric contract. Construct a path of at most **three** representable segments by replacing defining planes one at a time: `(s0,s1,s2) → (s0,s1,r2) → (s0,r1,r2) → (r0,r1,r2)`, choosing a valid order and clipping to the current AABB.
7. Apply f to front/back winding and emit/discard/reverse. Propagate source-polygon identity for attribute/provenance transfer. [Paper Figures 4–10 and §§4.1–4.4](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

DOCUMENTED optimizations: verified per-input no-self-intersection and no-nested-component flags allow skipping some local arrangements/classifications; operator-specific reachable winding sets allow early pruning. Splitting uses source-soup centers and AABB separation candidates, falling back to largest-variance splitting. Benchmark leaf threshold optimum was around 25 polygons. These are efficiency choices; special input flags are **preconditions**, not facts to infer from a valid-looking mesh. [Paper §4.5, Figures 11–12](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

## Robustness and guarantees

DOCUMENTED §§3.3, 6: import scales/rounds input to the 26-bit grid; paper describes approximately 15 nm coordinate resolution for a 1 m³ scene. All subsequent supported integer operations are exact. Export to float/double rounds again and **may introduce tiny self-intersections**. Repeated Booleans retain exactness only when reusing the internal plane/homogeneous representation, not exporting and reimporting floats between steps. [Paper](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

DOCUMENTED input must induce a piecewise-constant integer winding field. Arbitrary open meshes generally do not. Output is a **soup of convex polygons with T-junctions, no topology and no triangulation**; topological reconstruction is additional work. Accordingly exactness does not imply the emitted data is immediately a watertight manifold triangle mesh suitable for a slicer. [Paper §6](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

INFERRED: per-coordinate snapping error can be bounded from grid spacing, but near-contact topology can change under arbitrarily small perturbation. Do not equate an exact result for the snapped mesh with exact topology of the original analytic model. A F32-only import also cannot faithfully represent every 26-bit integer coordinate in one scalar: route integer quantization through F32x2 or exact decimal/dyadic conversion and U32, not `F32 -> large integer` after low bits have been lost.

DOCUMENTED exact tests validate heuristic interior-point/path proposals; fallback may use randomized offsets and repeated attempts. No deterministic uniform retry/work bound or universal runtime guarantee is provided. Wonky needs explicit bounded-work/refusal behavior and stable seeds if deterministic execution is required.

## Parallelism and performance

DOCUMENTED benchmark hardware: Intel i9-9900K, 3.60 GHz / 5 GHz boost; C++ Clang 12 -O3; multithreaded competitors and EMBER compared, with Mesh Arrangements compiled via GCC 10 as required. Algorithm implementation uses work-stealing threads, a synchronized task queue, memory reuse, specialized reduced-width integer operations and AABB prechecks. These are substantial performance ingredients, not just algorithmic asymptotics. [Paper §§4.5.4–5](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

DOCUMENTED author measurements, not locally reproduced:

- 1000 pairs from Thingi10K: **1.6 ms geometric mean** per Boolean with roughly 20k input faces, around 15 million input triangles/s; ~one order faster than fastest inexact competitor on that benchmark. Figure 13 includes methods with different guarantees; the float mesh-arrangement comparator measured self-intersection resolution, **not complete Boolean classification**.
- Figure 1: 1.2 million input triangles, **34 ms** on eight-core CPU versus 1010 ms QuickCSG and 141 s exact Mesh Arrangements.
- §5.3: about **5.5× speedup on eight cores**, then diminishing gains beyond physical cores due to memory bandwidth/hyperthreading; serial start-up limits short runs.
- Figure 25 stress case: 20 cubes with coincident top/bottom planes, only **240 triangles**, still **5.9 ms**; exact Mesh Arrangements 58280 ms, float arrangements 5620 ms. Triangle count alone is a poor complexity predictor.
- Figure 24: 20×20 beam intersection has quadratic output growth; EMBER **4.5 ms**, with correctness/performance comparisons shown. [Paper pp. 11–15](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

INFERRED Bend fit: local subproblems can be pure values and children joined with balanced fork-join, but geometric splitting does not guarantee balanced work or output size. Replace shared work queues/in-place arena reuse with owned affine-array partitions and bounded task batches. Group leaf tasks by polygon count and exact limb width for GPU; ragged BSP growth/path retries should use separate queues or CPU fallback. Eight-limb arithmetic is predictable GPU work, but U32 multiply/carry cost and transient polygon allocation may erase x64 speedups. Only a Bend benchmark can settle that.

## Known failures, limitations, war stories

- DOCUMENTED paper §6: floating import/export can reintroduce self-intersections; output has T-junctions and no ready topology; fixed-width representation is not closed under arbitrary point operations. These limitations directly affect whether a prototype's claimed exactness survives its output path.
- DOCUMENTED figures 23–25 deliberately exercise coplanarity, edge hits, overlap ownership, empty interior contacts and quadratic output. Include these crafted cases alongside random-pair throughput tests. [Paper](https://www.graphics.rwth-aachen.de/media/papers/339/ember_exact_mesh_booleans_via_efficient_and_robust_local_arrangements.pdf).

## Relevance for wonky

INFERRED high-value test/prototype plan:

1. **Arithmetic contract suite:** exact plane construction, parallel/coplanar distinction, three-plane intersection, denominator sign, point classification, support/edge-plane membership, clipping through vertices/edges, and overflow refusal. Generate near-bound positive/negative 26-bit coordinates, axis-aligned planes and almost parallel planes. Compare eight-limb primitives with a deliberately slower wider-limb implementation in Bend, preserving U32-only production constraints.
2. **Local invariants:** split children cover their parent without duplicated coplanar area; interior target is truly interior; traced winding is path-independent for independently valid paths; per-cell front/back values remain constant; total-order overlap owner does not lose summed transition weights. Exhaustively enumerate small integer-coordinate soups where an independent arrangement oracle is feasible.
3. **Benchmark manifest:** pin the verified 1000 JSON records and source mesh hashes/licenses; apply transforms with explicitly documented numeric semantics and quantize once to a shared grid. Avoid giving each contender a separately rounded mesh. Include raw and quantized bbox/volume/error metadata, unsupported-input reasons, wall-time/work budgets and output-size counts. No downloaded binary backend is needed.
4. **Add semantic oracles:** volume/set identities, point-membership witnesses away from boundaries, plane residuals, orientation, degeneracy/contact cases, idempotence and repeated-CSG invariants. Deterministic triangle order is a backend reproducibility check, not a correctness proof; different correct tessellations must compare geometrically.
5. **Budget the whole pipeline:** report import/quantization, arithmetic/classification, topology reconstruction, triangulation, analytic recovery and certified FDM mesh output separately. A 1.6 ms polygon-soup result is not comparable to a fully healed/triangulated analytic B-rep without accounting for missing stages.
6. **Hybrid boundary:** carry original analytic face/edge/provenance tags through every mesh split. EMBER decides topology of its snapped **planar** approximation; exact cylinder/cone intersections must still be recovered with analytic SSI and certified deviation checks. Small quantization error is not enough to justify a contact decision near a curved tangency. Explicitly refuse unresolved recovery.
7. **Persistent exact output:** do not throw away homogeneous coordinates/plane IDs between operations. Rendering/FDM export can round under a declared tolerance, but must revalidate watertightness and self-intersections after conversion. T-junction resolution and manifold assembly are mandatory downstream tasks, not supplied by EMBER's core guarantee.

## Verdict: adapt

Adapt the exact bounded-plane predicates, local arrangement/winding invariants and reproducible benchmark cases. They fit wonky's U32 arithmetic and local fork-join design unusually well, but require independent implementation, changed scheduling/memory strategy, semantic test oracles, and explicit output reconstruction. **Eight limbs are sufficient only inside the proved 26-bit plane-construction domain; the result is not automatically a watertight mesh or exact curved B-rep.** Open work: per-model data rights, U32 arithmetic proof/tests, and full-pipeline Bend performance measurement.
